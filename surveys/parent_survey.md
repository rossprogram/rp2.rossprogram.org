# Parent Survey

**Intro text shown to parents and guardians:**

> Thank you for being part of RP2's first term. Our goal is to deliver the best experience possible for your child, and to do that we need your feedback. Please take a couple of minutes to tell us how the program has started from your side.

Parents answer once per enrolled child.

---

### 1. How does your child talk about RP2?

- **Type:** single choice

- **Options:** Enthusiastically / Positively / Neutrally / Negatively / Rarely mentions it

- **Required:** yes

### 2. How is RP2 fitting alongside school and other commitments?

- **Type:** single choice

- **Options:** Very manageable / Manageable / Sometimes too much / Often too much

- **Required:** yes

### 3. How much do you agree with each statement?

- **Grid:** single choice

- **Columns:** Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neutral / Agree / Strongly agree

- **Rows:**

  - Enrollment and payment were straightforward

  - I get the information I need from the program

  - I know who to contact if there is a problem

  - I understand what my child is doing at RP2 and what a week in the program looks like

  - My child is enjoying the program

  - My child is learning a lot of math

- **Required:** no

### 4. Do you have any concerns about your child's experience that you would like us to know about?

- **Type:** long text

- **Required:** no

- **Helper text:** For anything you'd like us to follow up on privately, please email ross@rossprogram.org.
