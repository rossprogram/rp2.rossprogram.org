# ℝℙ² — Server Rules

These are the rules you and your parent or guardian signed before joining. The
full Code of Conduct and Participation Agreement are in the portal:
<https://rp2.rossprogram.org/agreements>

**Treat other people the way you would like to be treated.**

## Be respectful

- Show respect in speech and in all actions, to everyone, in every program space.
- No discrimination, harassment, or bullying — ever. Not on gender identity,
  nationality, race or ethnicity, religion, age, marital status, sexual
  orientation, or disability.
- No jokes, comments, or gestures that are discriminatory, demeaning, or
  inappropriate. No teasing, hazing, gossip, threats, or exclusion.
- Language you find acceptable may be offensive to someone else. Aim higher.

## Keep the space safe

- **Staff and participants do not hold private one-on-one DMs or video calls.**
  Questions belong in section channels, group channels, or office hours.
- No unwelcome, persistent, or targeted personal contact, including unwanted
  direct messages.
- No screenshots, photographs, or recordings of other participants or of
  program sessions without explicit permission.
- No indecent exposure, and no sexually explicit material.
- Don't disrupt sessions or program platforms.

## Do your own work

- Your problem set submissions must honestly represent your own understanding.
- Don't use the work of others — **including AI and LLM systems** — or present
  someone else's ideas as your own.
- Don't use unauthorized resources, like textbooks or websites, to find answers.
- **Collaboration is strongly encouraged.** It is far better to be puzzled by a
  hard problem and talk it through with someone than to look up the answer.

## Show up

- Attend your section's weekly problem session and office hours.
- Tell staff in advance if you have to miss one.
- Put consistent, genuine time into the problem sets each week.

## Keep the program inside the program

- Your account and your Zoom, Discord, and Gradescope access are **yours alone**.
  Don't share credentials.
- Don't share meeting links, invitations, problem sets, or solutions outside
  the program.

## Look after yourself, and each other

- Keep a healthy balance between the program, school, and rest.
- Tell staff about any health or medical concern that affects your participation.
- **Mental health concerns are health concerns.** If you feel stressed, anxious,
  down, or unable to take part, tell a member of staff. If you notice someone
  else struggling, tell us.
- Speak up about anything you see that worries you.

## Who's in charge

Ross staff run the program spaces, including this server and all Zoom sessions.
Follow instructions from instructors, teaching assistants, and other staff.

## If something goes wrong

If you ever feel harassed, bullied, or treated in a way that feels wrong —
whether it happened to you or you saw it happen to someone else — tell a member
of staff, or email **ross@rossprogram.org**. You can also make an anonymous
report or speak to the participant ombudsperson. Reports are handled promptly
and as confidentially as possible.

## Consequences

Breaking these rules can lead to a warning, loss of privileges, removal from
activities, your parent or guardian being contacted, or dismissal from the
program — at the discretion of the Ross Directors. On dismissal, access to all
program platforms ends immediately, and fees are not refunded.
