# Student Survey

**Intro text shown to students:**

> We want to know how RP2 is going for you so far. Please take a couple of minutes to fill out the following survey.

---

### 1. Which section are you in?

- **Type:** dropdown (prefilled with the section on the student's offer)

- **Options:** CGT-1 / CGT-2 / GGT-1 / GGT-2 / QUADRATIC-1 / QUADRATIC-2 / TOPOLOGY-1 / TOPOLOGY-2

- **Required:** yes

### 2. How much are you enjoying RP2 so far?

- **Type:** scale, 1 to 5

- **Labels:** 1 = Not at all, 5 = A lot

- **Required:** yes

### 3. The pace of my course is…

- **Type:** single choice

- **Options:** Too slow / About right / Too fast

- **Required:** yes

### 4. About how many hours do you spend on each problem set outside of live sessions?

- **Type:** single choice

- **Options:** Under 2 / 2–4 / 4–6 / 6–8 / 8–10 / More than 10

- **Required:** yes

### 5. How much do you agree with each statement?

- **Grid:** single choice

- **Columns:** Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neutral / Agree / Strongly agree

- **Rows:**

  - The problem sets lead me to discover new ideas

  - I feel comfortable asking questions on Zoom and Discord

  - My instructor explains things clearly

  - The feedback on Set 1 helped me understand what to improve

  - I talk to other students about the problems

  - Problem sessions are fun

  - Office hours are helpful

  - I wish meetings were longer

  - I would be interested in taking a second course in my topic

- **Required:** no

### 6. Does your section's meeting time still work for you?

- **Type:** single choice

- **Options:** Yes, it works well / Mostly, with occasional conflicts / No

- **Required:** yes

### 7. Is there anything you would like to share about your experience so far?

- **Type:** long text

- **Required:** no
